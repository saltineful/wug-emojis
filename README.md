# CC0 Wug Emojis
SVG wug emojis released into the public domain, exactly what it says on the tin. My initial intent was to make them in Twemoji style (and I may yet do that at a future date), so they are at the unpleasantly small Twemoji SVG size (36×36); they can easily be resized in a competent SVG editor.

They were made in [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/) based on the uppermost wug from [a sketch](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WugTest.png) by Jean Berko Gleason, the originator of the wug (along with several other pseudospecies which didn’t take off); this is PD-old/unrenewed. The files in the JBG directory are based on an autotrace of said uppermost wug; the files in the / directory (apart from the license and readme) are based on my hand-trace and are resultantly rather cleaner.

# Wugs Available
- clean trace:
    - **wug-blue.svg:** a very simple blue wug, with black lineart and a white margin around said lineart.
    - ***wugs with the conlang flag:*** precisely what they sound like. Black lineart with a purple background (and margin) and a black Tower of Babel atop a golden rising sun (slightly altered from the actual conlang flag due to the shape of the wug), tilted so the Tower points to the eye of the wug.
        - **wug-of-babel-with-black-eye:** A variant with the eye in black.
        - **wug-of-babel-with-yellow-eye:** A variant with the eye in yellow.
    - **wug-upvote:** A blue wug with a yellow rounded square in the bottom right corner containing a white up arrow. Both the square and the arrow have black outlines.
    - **wug-downvote:** Like the above, but with a white down arrow and a purple rounded square.
- ***Jean Berko Gleason wugs:***
    - **JBG-wug.svg:** the equivalent of wug-blue.svg, same description.
    - **JBG-wug-of-babel.svg:** exactly as it sounds.
